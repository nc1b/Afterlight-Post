# Afterlight Post live demo walkthrough

## Launch and controls

Extract the full Windows ZIP and open **Afterlight Post.exe**. In Unity **6000.6.9f1**, open `Assets/Scenes/Main.unity` and click **Play**. Press **Space** on the title screen. Choose **01 First Light** on the stamp screen with the arrow keys and press **Space** again.

Light every lamp in each street. A bell charges the shot and sends it back along its route. Striped mirrors flip when a shot returns past them, even after a miss. A lit lamp opens any matching gate.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W / A / S / D** | Walk up / left / down / right |
| **↑ / ← / ↓ / →** | Shoot in that direction |
| **Z** or **Backspace** | Undo the last shot, restoring its charge, the firing tile, and puzzle state |
| **R** | Restart the current street |
| **Space** or **Enter** | Start, advance after clearing, or fold a discovered note |
| **Esc** | Back to the stamp screen from play |
| **M / F11** | Toggle sound / fullscreen |

Tap each movement key once per tile and let each move and shot finish. Coordinates count from **(0, 0)** at the outside upper-left wall, with x increasing right and y increasing down. After each street, wait a beat after **STREET LIT!**, then press **Space** to advance.

## SPOILERS · All ten streets

### 01 First Light

1. From **(2, 4)**, tap **W, W, A** to **(1, 2)** at the left end of the lamp’s row.
2. Press **→** to ring the bell at **(10, 2)**. The return lights the lamp at **(6, 2)**. Press **Space** after the clear prompt.

### 02 Round the Corner

1. From **(2, 5)**, tap **W, A** to **(1, 4)**, lined up with the fixed mirror.
2. Press **→**. The mirror at **(8, 4)** banks the shot up to the bell at **(8, 1)**. The return lights both lamps at **(8, 2)** and **(4, 4)**. Press **Space**.

### 03 Return to Sender

1. From **(2, 4)**, tap **A** to **(1, 4)**. Press **→**. The striped mirror at **(9, 4)** sends the shot down into the wall and flips on the return.
2. Stay there and press **→** again. The flipped mirror banks up into the bell at **(9, 2)**. The return lights **(9, 3)**. Press **Space**.

### 04 Night Gate

1. From **(2, 4)**, tap **D** three times, then **W** to **(5, 3)**. Press **←**. The bell at **(2, 3)** charges the return through the brass lamp at **(4, 3)** and opens the gate.
2. Tap **D** three times, then **S** to **(8, 4)** on the right side. Press **→**. The fixed mirror at **(12, 4)** banks up to the bell at **(12, 2)**. The return lights **(10, 4)**. Press **Space**.

### 05 Harbour Lights

1. From **(1, 1)**, tap **S** six times and **D** twice to **(3, 7)** on the bottom dock. Press **↑**. The shot turns at **(3, 2)**, **(8, 2)**, and **(8, 4)** before ringing the centre bell at **(6, 4)**. The return lights **(6, 2)** and **(3, 4)**.
2. Tap **D** eight times and **W** once to **(11, 6)** on the right dock. Press **←**. The mirror at **(6, 6)** banks up to the same bell. The return lights **(9, 6)**. Press **Space**.

### 06 Both Sides

1. From **(3, 4)**, tap **A, A** to **(1, 4)**. Press **→** three times from that tile, waiting for each return. The first shot hits the lower wall and flips the mirror at **(6, 4)**. The second rings the top bell at **(6, 1)** and lights **(6, 2)**. The third hits the lower wall and sets the mirror for the next shot.
2. Tap **S** three times, **D** five times, then **W** to **(6, 6)**. Press **↑**. The same mirror now sends the shot right to the bell at **(11, 4)**. The return lights **(9, 4)**. Press **Space**.

### 07 Two Switches

1. From **(1, 7)**, tap **W** three times to **(1, 4)**. Press **→**. The shot turns down into the wall and flips the left striped mirror at **(6, 4)**.
2. Tap **S** three times and **D** five times to **(6, 7)**. Press **↑**. The two striped mirrors guide the shot to the right bell at **(10, 1)**. The return lights **(10, 2)** and flips both mirrors.
3. Tap **D** four times to **(10, 7)**. Press **↑**. The flipped pair sends the shot to the left bell at **(6, 1)**. The return lights **(6, 2)**. Press **Space**.

### 08 Relay

1. From **(1, 7)**, tap **D, D** to **(3, 7)**. Press **↑**. The shot rings the centre bell at **(6, 1)**, lights **(6, 2)** on the return, and flips the left and centre mirrors.
2. Tap **D** six times to **(9, 7)** and press **↑**. The shot rings the centre bell again and flips the centre and right mirrors.
3. Tap **A** three times to **(6, 7)**. Press **↑** twice from this tile, waiting for each return. The first shot reaches the right bell **(9, 1)** and lights **(9, 2)**. The second reaches the left bell **(3, 1)** and lights **(3, 2)**. Press **Space**.

### 09 Lock Keeper

1. From **(1, 7)**, tap **D, D** to **(3, 7)**. Press **↑**. The left mirror banks into the closed gate and flips on the return.
2. Tap **D** six times to **(9, 7)**. Press **↑** three times from this tile. Wait for each return. The first hits the closed gate and flips the right mirror. The second rings the side bell at **(11, 4)** and opens the gate by lighting **(10, 4)**. The third goes through the open gate to the left bell at **(3, 1)** and lights **(3, 2)**.
3. Tap **A** six times to **(3, 7)**. Press **↑**. The shot goes through the open gate, turns up to the bell at **(9, 1)**, and lights **(9, 2)**. Press **Space**.

### 10 Home Signal

1. From **(1, 7)**, tap **D, D** to **(3, 7)**. Press **↑**. The shot banks into the closed gate and flips the left mirror.
2. Tap **D** six times to **(9, 7)**. Press **↑** three times from here, waiting for each return. The first hits the closed gate and flips the middle-right mirror. The second rings the side bell at **(11, 4)** and lights **(10, 4)** to open the row gate. The third reaches the left bell at **(3, 1)** and lights **(3, 2)**, opening the gate on the bottom dock.
3. Tap **D** four times to **(13, 7)**. Press **↑**. The rightmost striped mirror at **(13, 5)** banks the shot into the wall and flips.
4. Stay there and press **↑** again. The shot turns at **(13, 5)**, **(6, 5)**, and **(6, 2)** before ringing the far-right bell at **(13, 2)**. The return lights **(11, 2)** and **(9, 2)**.
5. When **ALL LIT!** appears, wait a beat and press **Space**. The ending reads **THE HARBOUR IS LIT AGAIN.** The post office windows and doorway shine onto the dock. Press **Space** again to return to the stamp screen.

## SPOILERS · Optional mailboxes

The mailboxes open on the return leg of a bank shot that ends at a wall. Each costs one shot and fits within that street’s original charge budget. A mailbox flashes, plays a chime, and shows a note. **Space** folds it. **Z** can undo the mailbox shot, and **R** resets its street.

### Round the Corner · Undelivered letter

1. Before clearing the street, tap **D** nine times from the spawn **(2, 5)** to the torn slip at **(11, 5)**. Stand on it to read the hint about banking a shot into brick.
2. Tap **A** three times to the **up arrow scratched at (8, 5)** directly below the mirror. Press **↑**. The mirror banks the shot right past the mailbox at **(10, 4)** to the wall. The mailbox opens on the return.
3. Press **Space** to fold the letter. Tap **A** seven times, then **W** to **(1, 4)**. Press **→** for the main shot and **Space** to continue.

### Night Gate · Dock postcard

1. Take the first main-route shot from **(5, 3)** to light the brass lamp and open the gate. From your firing tile, tap **D** eight times, then **S, S** to the tag at **(13, 5)**. Stand on it to read the hint.
2. Tap **W** once to **(13, 4)** and stand on the scratch that points left at the mirror. Press **←**. The mirror banks the shot down through the mailbox at **(12, 5)** into the lower wall. The postcard appears on the return.
3. Press **Space** to fold it. Tap **W**, **A** five times, then **S** to **(8, 4)**. Press **→** for the remaining lamp and **Space** to continue.
