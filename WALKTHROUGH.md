# Afterlight Post live demo walkthrough

## Launch, controls, and objective

Extract the full Windows demo ZIP and open **Afterlight Post.exe**. To run from source, open the repository in Unity **6000.6.9f1**, open `Assets/Scenes/Main.unity`, and click **Play**. Press **Space** at the title screen.

Light all four lamps across **Receiving**, **Sorting Room**, and **Dispatch**. Mirrors turn the pulse. A bell sends it back along its incoming route, and the bright return pulse lights any lamp it crosses.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W / A / S / D** | Move up / left / down / right |
| **↑ / ← / ↓ / →** | Shoot in that direction |
| **Z** or **Backspace** | Undo the last shot and return to the tile you fired from |
| **R** | Restart the current room with full shots |
| **Space** or **Enter** | Start, advance after a clear, or fold the letter |
| **Esc** | Return to the title from a room, or close an open letter |
| **M / F11** | Toggle sound / fullscreen |

Tap movement keys once per tile. Let each move and shot finish before the next input. The coordinates in the walkthrough count right from the left edge and down from the top edge, with the outer wall starting at **(0, 0)**.

## SPOILERS · Main route

### 1. Receiving

1. From the starting tile **(2, 6)**, tap **W, W**. Stand at **(2, 4)**, in the left aisle on the same row as the mirror.
2. Press **→**. The pulse hits the mirror at **(9, 4)** and turns up to the bell at **(9, 2)**. The returning pulse lights the lamp at **(9, 3)**. The room warms and its top windows light up.
3. When **ROOM LIT!** appears, wait a beat and press **Space**.

### 2. Sorting Room

1. From the starting tile **(2, 7)**, tap **W**. Stand at **(2, 6)**, in the left aisle level with the lower of the two left mirrors.
2. Press **→**. The lower mirror at **(5, 6)** turns the pulse up. The upper mirror at **(5, 2)** turns it right toward the upper-right bell at **(12, 2)**. The return crosses and lights the lamp at **(9, 2)**.
3. When **ROOM LIT!** appears, wait a beat and press **Space**.

### 3. Dispatch

1. From the starting tile **(2, 7)**, tap **W** to stand at **(2, 6)**. Press **→**. The mirror at **(5, 6)** turns the pulse up to the left bell at **(5, 2)**. The return lights the brass lamp at **(5, 4)** and opens the shutter on the bottom aisle.
2. Tap **S** once, then **D** nine times. Walk through the open shutter and stop at **(11, 7)**, directly below the left mirror of the pair on the right side.
3. Press **↑**. The mirror at **(11, 5)** turns the pulse right and the mirror at **(13, 5)** turns it up to the far-right bell at **(13, 1)**. The return lights the blue lamp at **(13, 3)**.
4. When **ALL LIT!** appears, wait a beat and press **Space**. The ending shows **THE POST OFFICE IS OPEN.** The front door and windows shine onto the dock. Press **Space** again to return to the title.

## SPOILERS · Optional undelivered letter

Do this in the **Sorting Room** before lighting its main lamp.

1. Find the **torn slip** at **(13, 7)**, the far-right end of the bottom aisle. From the starting tile, tap **D** eleven times. Standing on the slip displays **SEND IT BACK THE WAY IT CAME. THE DEAD BOX OPENS ON THE RETURN FROM A BELL.**
2. Find the **crooked arrow** scratched into the floor at **(11, 6)**, one tile below the lower-right mirror. From the slip, tap **A, A** to stand at **(11, 7)**. The arrow one tile above you points up toward that mirror.
3. Press **↑**. The mirror at **(11, 5)** banks the pulse left past the dead mailbox at **(9, 5)**, then into the lower bell at **(7, 5)**. On the return, the mailbox flashes and opens. The **UNDELIVERED LETTER** appears.
4. Press **Space** to fold the letter. Tap **A** nine times to return to the room’s starting tile **(2, 7)**, then use the main-route **W, →, Space** steps to finish Sorting. Finish Dispatch as above. The ending adds **ONE LOST LETTER FOUND.**
